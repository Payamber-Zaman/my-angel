The Love Letter

My dearest Noor, my sweet angel...

I don’t think you know how much you mean to me. You are gentle and funny and so wonderfully yourself, and being near you, even through a screen, feels like coming home.

Thank you for your patience, your kindness and your laugh. You make my days lighter just by being in them, and I notice every little thing you do.

I cherish our small moments most: the silly chats, the games, the quiet in between. And I’m so excited for every memory we haven’t made yet.

If you ever doubt how loved you are, read this again. I mean every word, and I’ll keep meaning them.

Forever your biggest admirer, your clingiest person, and your favourite idiot. ♡

If there’s one place I’d choose to be, in every lifetime, it would be beside you.
